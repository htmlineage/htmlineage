Paid social 30-day retention rose to 46.6% in August from July's 41.7%, a relative gain of 11.8%, and ranked #1 of the four channels.

Paid social brought in 31.2% of August's new customers, so its gain lifts retention across the whole base, not just one corner of it.

Paid social's gain is likely to hold into September.
